Interview: Sarah Chen, freelance brand designer
Date: Oct 14
Contact: sarah.chen.design@gmail.com, (415) 555-0142

Q: Walk me through how you get paid today.
Sarah: I send a PDF invoice from a template, then I keep a spreadsheet of who's paid. Honestly the spreadsheet is a mess.

Q: What's the most frustrating part?
Sarah: I hate chasing late payments. I wish it reminded clients for me. I'll finish a logo, send the invoice, and then three weeks later I'm writing this awkward "just checking in!" email. It makes me feel like a debt collector, not a designer.

Q: How often does that happen?
Sarah: Maybe a third of my clients pay late. One client took 70 days last spring.

Q: If you could wave a wand?
Sarah: Automatic reminders. A polite nudge at 7 days overdue, then another at 14. I never want to write that email again.

Q: Anything else?
Sarah: Mostly that. Everything else about invoicing is fine.
