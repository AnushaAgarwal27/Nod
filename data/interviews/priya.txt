Interview: Priya Raman, freelance wedding and event photographer
Date: Oct 16
Contact: priya@priyaraman.photo, 646-555-0187

Q: Tell me about your clients.
Priya: I shoot destination weddings, so it's very international. Half my clients are in Europe: Italy, Portugal, Germany.

Q: What's hard about invoicing them?
Priya: I need euro invoices. Half my clients are in Europe. Right now I invoice in dollars and the couples have to work out the conversion, and some of them get charged fees by their bank. It looks unprofessional.

Q: How do you handle it today?
Priya: I make a second invoice by hand in euros using whatever the exchange rate is that day. It takes forever and I've made mistakes.

Q: What about late payments?
Priya: Not really a problem for me. Couples pay a deposit up front.

Q: One thing you'd change?
Priya: Let me pick the currency per client. Euros, pounds, dollars.
