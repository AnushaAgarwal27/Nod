Interview: Tom Becker, independent strategy consultant
Date: Oct 20 (Round 2, upload after the restart)
Contact: tom.becker@beckerconsulting.de, +49 30 5550 1234

Q: How do you invoice?
Tom: Monthly retainers. Most of my clients are in Germany and the Netherlands, a few in the US.

Q: Have you used tools with automatic payment reminders?
Tom: Yes, and honestly auto-reminders annoy my clients. My clients are big companies with 60-day payment terms. If a robot emails their finance team on day 7, it looks pushy and it hurts the relationship. I turned it off immediately in the last tool I used.

Q: So no reminders at all?
Tom: Not by default. Make reminders optional, off unless I turn them on. Maybe for small clients. It should be my choice per client.

Q: What do you actually need?
Tom: I need euro invoices. Most of my retainers are in euros and I'm stuck converting by hand. Multi-currency is the thing I'd pay for.

Q: Anything else?
Tom: Make sure the invoice shows the right currency symbol and VAT line. That matters in Europe.
